    Diagnosis, payment and refunds: details

    
Payment and refund policy

    • Cleaning is a separate service without fault diagnosis or an inspection fee.
    • Services other than standalone diagnosis: 50% of the agreed price at intake and the balance on return.
    • Standalone fault diagnosis: 150 EGP paid in full at intake, not in two instalments. It is credited toward an agreed repair of that same fault, without charging it twice.
    • We do not automatically deduct an inspection fee from a cleaning or other service deposit. Any separate diagnosis requires agreement on its cost before it is performed.
    • Parts are priced separately. Imported parts are paid for in full before ordering.
    

    
Refunds and cancellations4 situations · Open to read each oneDetails

    

    • If the service is not possible or the provider cannot perform it, payment for work not performed is refunded.
    • Agreed standalone diagnosis that has been completed costs 150 EGP for its findings, whether or not you proceed with a repair.
    • If you change your mind before work or diagnosis starts, the service payment is refunded. If work has started, we explain what was completed and the amount due before settling the balance.
    • If imported parts do not arrive, payment for those undelivered parts is refunded.
    
Refund amounts are confirmed on WhatsApp. You can acknowledge receipt with a message.

    Questions before you start?
    

    
Is this a retail repair shop?

    No. This is a home-based service, not a retail shop. We arrange a suitable location and time for handover and return.
    

    

    
What types of repairs do you offer?

    We offer maintenance, cleaning and clearly scoped repairs. Advanced motherboard or circuit repairs are not currently offered. We explain if a service is not possible before committing to repair.
    

    

    
Could my data or game saves be lost?

    Some service work carries a risk of data loss. Back up important data before handover. Data backup is not included unless separately agreed.
    

    

    
Is full payment required in advance?

    Standalone fault diagnosis is paid in full: 150 EGP at intake. Other services are split: 50% at intake and the balance on return. Cleaning has no inspection fee. Imported parts are paid for in full in advance.
